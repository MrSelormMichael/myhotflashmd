About Us

Care built by someone who gets it.

my hotflash MD exists because menopause care deserves better — more time, more honesty, and more options than a rushed ten-minute visit.

Our Story

Why we started my hotflash MD

Millions of women move through perimenopause and menopause every year, and too many are told to just push through it. my hotflash MD was built to change that — offering real, evidence-based treatment options delivered with the time and attention this stage of life deserves.

We believe you shouldn't have to become your own advocate just to get taken seriously. Every visit is designed to listen first, then build a plan around your body, your symptoms, and your goals.
